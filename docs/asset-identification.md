# Asset Identification

Asset identification is the foundation of cybersecurity risk assessment. The organization first creates an inventory of assets, records ownership and business purpose, and evaluates the importance of each asset.

## Asset Categories
Hardware, Software, Data, Network, Cloud Service, Digital Asset, Credential, People.

## CIA Assessment
Each asset receives 1–5 ratings for:
- Confidentiality: consequence of unauthorized disclosure.
- Integrity: consequence of unauthorized modification.
- Availability: consequence of loss of access.

Formula:

`Criticality Score = (C + I + A) / 3`

Classification:
- 4.00–5.00: Critical
- 3.00–3.99: High
- 2.00–2.99: Medium
- 1.00–1.99: Low

These thresholds are project-defined methodology and will be validated during case studies.
